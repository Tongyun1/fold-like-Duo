# fold-like-Duo v1.0.1

降低半合盖静止状态下的不必要截屏与渲染开销，针对 [#1](https://github.com/Tongyun1/fold-like-Duo/issues/1) 的耗能反馈。

## 更新内容

- 静止约 0.75 秒后，实时截屏上限从 60 fps 降至 5 fps；再次移动时恢复高帧率。
- 相同角度和外观参数不再反复唤醒 Metal 渲染，减少设置界面的重复刷新。
- 静止时降低传感器轮询频率，休眠时停止轮询。
- 修复覆盖层隐藏后仍被延迟截图唤醒、截屏启动与停止竞态，以及渲染信号量未释放的问题。

真实 Metal 回归测试中，120 次相同参数更新产生的额外 GPU 帧由旧版的 93 帧降至 0 帧。该结果验证了重复渲染已消除，不代表已测得电池续航或温度改善幅度。静止时桌面内容最多每约 200 毫秒更新一次。

## 安装

需要 macOS 14 或更高版本；DMG 内为支持 Apple 芯片与 Intel 的 Universal 2 应用。退出旧版后，打开 DMG，将应用拖入“应用程序”文件夹并替换旧版。

此构建尚未经过 Apple 公证。首次打开若被 macOS 阻止，请前往“系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 安全性 → 仍要打开”。实时特效需要屏幕录制权限。

## English

This update reduces unnecessary capture and rendering work while the lid is held partially closed, addressing the energy-use report in #1. Capture falls from up to 60 fps to 5 fps after approximately 0.75 seconds at rest and returns to full rate when the lid moves. Unchanged parameters no longer wake Metal rendering; sensor polling slows at rest and stops during sleep. Capture shutdown and hidden-overlay rendering races are also fixed.

Both arm64 and x86_64 builds, bundle checks, and Metal regressions pass. In the stationary-input regression, 120 identical updates produced 93 extra GPU frames before the fix and zero afterward. Battery life and temperature improvements have not been measured.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Quit the previous version, open the DMG, and replace the app in Applications. This build is not Apple-notarized; see the first-launch instructions above or in the README.
